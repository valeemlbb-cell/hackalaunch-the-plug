We are launching Kilat, a savings app for Indonesian workers in their first or second job.
You set a goal, we round up every transaction and move the change into a locked pocket you
cannot raid on a Friday night. Bahasa Indonesia only. The people we want are 18-24, just
started earning, already watching budgeting content on TikTok and YouTube.

Goal for this campaign is signups on the waitlist before the public launch, and we care
about cost per signup more than raw views.

Total budget is $3,000. We can pay around $350 per creator. We want 2 x TikTok short and
1 x YouTube video per creator, running 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-21.

No gambling content, and every paid post has to be labelled as an ad. We would rather work
with smaller accounts that people actually trust than buy one big name.
